Fresh off a $300M raise, Indian AI startup Sarvam launched Indus — a full-stack sovereign AI platform built around a 100B-parameter model trained entirely in India, explicitly positioned as an alternative to Anthropic and Microsoft Copilot for enterprises wary of foreign AI dependency. Initial focus is digital-native companies and developers, with enterprise integrations planned once ROI is proven. Government alignment is central to the strategy — a real, live example of the "national sovereign AI stack" pattern, not just an announcement.
