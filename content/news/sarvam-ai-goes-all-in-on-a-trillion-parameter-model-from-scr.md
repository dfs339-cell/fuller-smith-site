Announced at Epoch 2026 in Bengaluru, Sarvam AI laid out a concrete roadmap — roughly 2 months of pre-training, 4–6 months total including reinforcement learning, on ~10,000 Blackwell GPUs — backed by senior hires poached from Mistral and xAI. Alongside the flagship model, Sarvam launched an on-premise defense-grade stack ("Chanakya") and consumer/enterprise agent products. This is India's clearest declaration yet that "sovereign AI" means building from zero, not just fine-tuning someone else's open weights.
