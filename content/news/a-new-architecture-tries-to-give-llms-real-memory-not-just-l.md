A project called METIS proposes building memory directly into the transformer architecture itself, rather than relying purely on context-window tricks or external vector databases. Early-stage and unproven at scale, but conceptually adjacent to the episodic-memory approach Hermes Agent takes with its three-layer system — worth watching as a signal that "agent memory" is becoming an architecture-level concern, not just a bolt-on.
