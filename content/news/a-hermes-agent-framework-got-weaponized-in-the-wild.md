Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 traced a Zhuhai-based threat actor who wired DeepSeek into the open-source Hermes Agent framework and directed it via Telegram to autonomously scan and attack 460+ internet-facing systems, with confirmed breaches on three Citrix NetScaler devices. A sharp real-world reminder that the same self-directed agent architecture making Hermes interesting for legitimate automation is equally usable for offense — governance isn't optional once an agent framework goes autonomous.
