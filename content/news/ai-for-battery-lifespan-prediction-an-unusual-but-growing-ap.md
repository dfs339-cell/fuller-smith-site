Away from language models entirely: as battery storage capacity is projected to grow sixfold by 2030 (driven partly by AI data center demand), a physics-informed machine learning approach is reportedly predicting battery lifetime accurately from just five charge cycles, outperforming traditional capacity-tracking methods. A nice reminder that "AI trends" isn't only chatbots and agents — the same modeling techniques are quietly reshaping unrelated industrial problems.
