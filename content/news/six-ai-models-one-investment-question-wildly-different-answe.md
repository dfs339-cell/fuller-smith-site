An experiment asking six models (including Sonnet 5, Opus 5, and GPT-5.6) how much of a portfolio to allocate to the Japanese yen produced recommendations ranging from 0% to 7.5% — a useful, concrete demonstration that model "agreement" on financial judgment calls is far from guaranteed, even when the underlying reasoning process looks consistent. Good ammunition against treating any single model's financial output as authoritative.
