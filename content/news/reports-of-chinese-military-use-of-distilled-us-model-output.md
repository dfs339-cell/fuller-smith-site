Reporting describes Chinese defense researchers, including PLA Unit 96941, using model distillation — training smaller domestic models on the outputs of US systems like GPT-3.5 — to build lightweight AI for drone navigation, target recognition, and cyber operations, sidestepping chip export restrictions entirely by not needing the compute the original model required. A concrete illustration of why "export controls on hardware" and "export controls on capability" are turning out to be very different problems.
