A builder documented assembling a three-RTX-3090 AI server (72GB combined VRAM) on an AMD Threadripper Pro workstation platform, framing the entire project around resilience against cloud pricing shifts, rate limiting, model deprecation, and geopolitical export controls — not just cost savings. A clean, concrete example of the "own your inference" logic playing out at real hobbyist/prosumer scale, using older but still capable hardware rather than the latest cards.
