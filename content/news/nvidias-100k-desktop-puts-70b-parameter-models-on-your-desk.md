NVIDIA announced the DGX Station — a desktop workstation with 748GB of unified memory and the new GB200 Grace Blackwell Ultra chip, enabling full-precision 70B-parameter inference locally, no cloud round-trip. ARM CPU and GPU sit on one NVLink-connected package, eliminating the usual data-copy overhead. "Trillion-parameter" marketing claims require heavy 4-bit quantization to actually fit — worth reading skeptically — but at ~$100K, compared against $70K+/month in equivalent cloud inference spend, the ROI math for high-frequency enterprise workloads is real. Linux ships first; Windows support is slated for Q4.
