Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 release has intensified US federal debate over how to regulate open-weight exports. Notably, over 230 companies — including Nvidia, Microsoft, and Meta — signed a letter opposing restrictions on open-weight models, while Anthropic pointedly did not sign, instead pushing for capability-based rather than openness-based regulation. One useful nuance: despite being "open," Kimi K3 is really only usable by organizations with serious infrastructure — openness and accessibility are turning out to be two different things.
