LG AI Research released K-EXAONE 2.0, a 750-billion-parameter model, open-sourced under Apache 2.0 — the second model under South Korea's government-backed Independent AI Foundation Model Project. Despite the size, it only activates around 37 billion parameters per token thanks to its Mixture-of-Experts design, keeping inference costs closer to a much smaller model. It landed just two days after SK Telecom's rival A.X K2, making this a genuine two-horse domestic race, not a one-off release — Korea's answer to the India/UAE sovereign-AI moves already on the radar.
