A new open-weights model family called Opt.Gear targets the extreme edge — dense models as small as 1M and 270M parameters alongside a 1B version, with a novel hybrid architecture cutting KV-cache memory growth for long-context use. The headline claim: Opt.Gear-1M runs at 20 tokens/second on an ARM Cortex-M7 microcontroller — genuinely tiny hardware, not a phone or a laptop. A good illustration of just how wide the deployment spectrum has become, from this up to NVIDIA's 748GB desktop above.
